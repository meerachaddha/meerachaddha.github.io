## Section 3: Salary by Education Level

### Scatter - Bachelor's or Lower
Jobs requiring a Bachelor's degree or lower show salary clusters between $50k–$150k, with the highest density at 0–5 years of experience. The distribution thins out significantly beyond 5 years, suggesting fewer postings require extensive experience for this education group.

### Scatter - Master's or PhD
Master's and PhD roles show a slightly wider salary range, with more postings in the $100k–$200k band. The distribution thins beyond 5 years, but high-salary outliers ($300k+) appear at mid-range experience levels.

### Histogram - Bachelor's or Lower
The salary distribution is strongly right-skewed, with the majority of postings concentrated between $80k–$150k. The long tail indicates a small number of high-paying outlier roles.

### Histogram - Master's or PhD
Master's and PhD distributions show a slightly broader spread with a secondary bump around $200k–$250k, suggesting a distinct cluster of higher-paying specialized roles.

## Section 4: Salary by Remote Work Type

### Scatter - Remote
Remote roles show salaries between $50k–$200k with a slight upward trend at higher experience levels. The spread widens beyond 5 years, suggesting more variability in pay for senior remote positions.

### Scatter - Hybrid
Hybrid roles have far fewer postings, with salaries mostly between $50k–$150k concentrated at 0–5 years. Hybrid arrangements appear less common for senior-level positions.

### Scatter - Onsite
Onsite roles dominate the dataset with dense clusters between $50k–$200k. The distribution extends to 15 years, reflecting onsite being the traditional work arrangement with broader experience ranges.

### Histogram - All Remote Types
All three work types show right-skewed distributions peaking around $100k–$150k. Hybrid roles have a narrower peak while remote and onsite show broader distributions. Remote roles skew slightly higher overall.
